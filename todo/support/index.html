Common questions

Where are my lists stored?

On your device only. SimplyDone has no account and no servers, so no one else can see your lists, not even the developer.

Are my lists backed up?

When your iPhone or iPad is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, SimplyDone's data is included in that backup. Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep a backup if you want to keep your lists.

How many lists can I make for free?

The free version keeps up to 3 lists with up to 3 tasks in each. Archived lists count toward the limit. Lists in Recently Deleted don't. SimplyDone Pro removes the limits.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep for good.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

In SimplyDone, go to Settings → SimplyDone Pro and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications → SimplyDone, and turn on Allow Notifications. Also check that a Focus mode isn't silencing them.

I deleted a list by mistake.

Go to Settings → Recently Deleted in SimplyDone. Deleted lists stay there for 30 days, and you can restore them.

Can I change the language?

Yes, in Settings → Language. SimplyDone is available in English, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese and Simplified Chinese.
