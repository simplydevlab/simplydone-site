1.2.0

Update · Oct 6, 2026

Improved

  • The free version now keeps up to 5 lists, with up to 10 tasks in each.

Update · Oct 5, 2026

Improved

  • Tap ⓘ beside the total on the card at the top of the main screen to see which tasks it counts.

Fixed

  • A repeating list in Archived or Recently Deleted stays as you left it, and catches up on its repeats when you bring it back.

Release · Oct 5, 2026

New

  • See how many tasks you finish each day, week or month, and your streak, on a card at the top of the main screen. Pick a range or a group from its menu. With Pro.
  • Swipe the card left or right to hide it, and turn it back on in Settings › Progress.
  • The SimplyDone Pro page shows what Free and Pro each include.

Improved

  • Checking off a task gives a little pop and a tap, and a finished list shows a check in its ring.

Fixed

  • Info bubbles show their whole text and close when you tap outside them.
  • If you finish a pinned list by mistake, uncheck a task and it goes back to Pinned.

1.1.0

Update · Oct 4, 2026

Improved

  • A weekly list can repeat on several days, such as Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Release · Oct 4, 2026

New

  • Add a description to any task, shown under its name.
  • Share a list with AirDrop. If you already have one with the same name, replace it or keep both.
  • Delete all your data from Settings.
  • What's New in Settings shows what changed in each version.
  • Repeat a list every day, every week or every 2 weeks: on each repeat day, its done tasks start fresh. With Pro.
  • A repeating list keeps its history: open History at the foot of the list to see what got done each day or week, with times, descriptions and photos. The current one is on top and updates as you go.
  • Duplicate a list, and change its name or anything else before it's made. Choose whether its tasks' descriptions and photos come along.
  • See when each list was created and last updated, at the bottom of the list.
  • Filter the main screen by group, to see just that group's lists. It works together with Pinned, Active and Completed.

Fixed

  • Closing a list's actions while a menu like Archive List is open no longer makes the buttons blink.
  • A new task's photos and reminder now have the same spacing as a saved task's.
